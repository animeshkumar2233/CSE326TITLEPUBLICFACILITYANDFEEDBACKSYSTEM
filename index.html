Citizen Submission Guidelines

  • Be Specific: Mention physical landmarks or building numbers to assist inspection teams.
  • Rating Standards: Assign a rating from 1 (severely damaged/hazardous) to 5 (optimal/satisfactory).
  • Instant Code: A reference tracking code is generated immediately upon submission.
  • Privacy: Contact information is securely used only for incident follow-up and verification.
